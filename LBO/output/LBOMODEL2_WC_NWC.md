# Working capital — NWC does change

## Why 0% / 0% was wrong
Growth and AI collections both move NWC. Zeroing the plug ignored receivables investment; zeroing the AI improve ignored AR-automation DSO cuts.

## Drivers (Assumptions_Drivers)

| Cell | Driver | Value | Source math |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **C25** | WC base % of Δrev | **2.5%** | Midpoint of VeloraAI SaaS/Software **NWC % of Sales 0–5%** |
| **C13** | WC / receivables improvement (AI) | **10%** | Global PayEx **~6 DSO days** ÷ Fairview B2B SaaS median **59 days** ≈ 10% (conservative vs 20–40% headlines) |

## Equation
- Base: `ΔNWC = ΔRevenue × 2.5%`
- AI: `ΔNWC = ΔRevenue × 2.5% × (1 − 10%)`

Conservative notes: positive 2.5% treats growth WC as a **use of cash** (ZI actually runs deferred revenue > AR). 10% improve is below “Cut DSO 20%+” / 30–40% automation claims.
