# CapEx reduction — AI-SDR reevaluation

## Why 0% was wrong
SaaS CapEx is mostly **employee gear**. Replacing SDRs with AI removes laptop/desk CapEx for those roles, so a 0% cut was unrealistic.

## Sources
1. **SaaSDB:** CapEx is *“primarily laptops, office equipment, and minor leasehold improvements.”*  
   https://saasdb.app/learn/financials/fcf-margin/
2. **ZoomInfo FY24 10-K:** *“had 3,508 employees”* incl. *“1,513 in sales and marketing”* (S&M ≈ **43%** of HC).  
   https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1794515/000179451525000045/zi-20241231.htm
3. **Digital Applied / Bridge Group:** net SDR headcount **down 18% YoY** (same cut as payroll/AI roles).

## Math (enters EQ)
`CapEx_cut ≈ 18% × (1,513 / 3,508) ≈ 7.8% → **8%**`  
`CapEx_AI = Revenue × 2% × (1 − 8%)`

## Driver
Assumptions_Drivers!C12 = **8%**
